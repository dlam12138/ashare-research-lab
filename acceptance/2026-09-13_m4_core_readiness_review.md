## Decision and smallest next task

M4-A's synthetic architecture is strong enough for a focused portability and configurability acceptance task. The next bounded task should demonstrate **two structurally distinct, legal synthetic hypotheses** through the existing public entry without editing core code; compare canonical contract/plan/dataset/matrix identities and the intended execution semantics across fresh processes, directories and the existing Ubuntu/Windows CI matrix; and document which envelope fields intentionally depend on the numeric runtime. It should retain all current quality, rank, PIT, identity, no-I/O and no-holdout gates. This is a proposed task, not started by this review.

After that acceptance, a separate North-Star review can decide whether to authorize design of a real daily-data adapter and its source/calendar/membership/PIT evidence contract. A real candidate dataset, literature acquisition, any new A-share outcome read, holdout or real hypothesis execution each remain outside this review. Synthetic test success cannot be promoted into a real mechanism finding.
