## Frozen methodology

- Core identity: `ROIC = NOPAT / average invested capital`.
- Primary candidate: operating-profit bridge with financing-view invested capital.
- NOPAT is not net profit, parent-attributable profit, EBITDA, a manual balancing plug, or an LLM adjustment.
- Direct operating tax is required. Reported ETR, statutory rate, clipping, and silent tax fallback are not canonical substitutes.
- Invested capital includes scope-matched parent equity, NCI, debt and leases, and subtracts only qualifying non-operating assets with evidence. Total liabilities and generic debt are rejected.
- Cash is classified by purpose and is never silently all subtracted. Associates/JVs, goodwill and leases have explicit matched policies.
- Average balance requires opening and closing balances; FY2021 therefore requires FY2020. PIT uses maximum `available_at`; a superseding restatement replaces the earlier version.
- Arithmetic remains Decimal precision 28 with round-half-even and the existing canonical quantum.

The frozen contract is in `docs/value_evaluation_methodology_roic_v1.md`, `config/value_evaluation_methodology_roic_v1.json`, and `docs/roic_input_contract.md`.
