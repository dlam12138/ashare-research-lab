# ROIC minimum official-fact acquisition plan v3

This plan is limited to FY2024 duration inputs, FY2023 opening balances,
FY2024 closing balances and official facts required to execute the already
frozen non-operating-asset policy. It does not authorize FY2020–FY2025 bulk
collection, a shadow calculation, a production ROIC result or scoring.

## A — primary formula direct facts

- FY2024 finance cost excluding lease interest
- FY2024 investment income
- FY2024 fair-value net change
- FY2024 asset-disposal gain/loss
- FY2024 direct operating-tax expense

## B — scope matching direct facts

- FY2024 lease-interest expense
- FY2023/FY2024 NCI
- FY2023/FY2024 associate investment
- FY2023/FY2024 joint-venture investment

## C — methodology supporting facts

- FY2023/FY2024 restricted cash
- FY2023/FY2024 officially proven non-operating financial assets

The classification policy itself is not an acquisition item. Cash and cash
equivalents is already ready and is not reacquired. The policy retains
unproven assets in invested capital, so it never manufactures a plug.

## D — secondary reconciliation/sensitivity

The minimum blocking batch has no D-layer acquisition items. Other
non-operating income/expense, profit before tax, total assets, goodwill and
non-interest-bearing operating liabilities remain explicit secondary roles
and do not block primary feasibility.

`validate_acquisition_plan(...)` is the sole authority for coverage and the
next-stage acquisition decision. A hand-written `ALLOWED` statement has no
effect.
