# PetroChina 2021—2025 Financial Safety Vertical Slice

Stage 2E-B records a transparent, descriptive financial-safety layer for
PetroChina (`601857.SH`). The evidence path is offline and dual-source: the
company annual report and the exchange-hosted annual report are checked against
the same direct CAS consolidated lines. Amounts are normalized from RMB million
to 万元 by ×100.

## Stage 2E-B.1 current-portion correction

The prior vertical slice treated the statement aggregate
`一年内到期的非流动负债` as an eligible current-portion Fact. That audit trail
is retained as rejected evidence. The five annual-report notes separately
disclose current long-term borrowings, current bonds, current lease liabilities,
and current long-term payables. Only the first three are interest-bearing; the
fourth is explicitly excluded. All five years have dual-source proof and use
`DERIVE_INTEREST_BEARING_CURRENT_PORTION_001 v1` to sum the three reconciled
components. FY2023 lease-component comparative evidence creates the only
current-portion derived v2 (`11,757,400` → `11,758,100` 万元).

## Definitions

Rule 006 directly reconciles the six base safety concepts plus these three
note-component concepts:

- `total_liabilities`
- `short_term_borrowings`
- `current_portion_of_long_term_borrowings`
- `current_portion_of_bonds_payable`
- `current_portion_of_lease_liabilities`
- `long_term_borrowings`
- `bonds_payable`
- `lease_liabilities`
- `cash_and_cash_equivalents`

The four version-1, descriptive-only metrics are:

- `asset_liability_ratio = total_liabilities / total_assets`
- `gross_interest_bearing_debt = short_term_borrowings + current_portion + long_term_borrowings + bonds_payable + lease_liabilities`
- `cash_coverage_of_interest_bearing_debt = cash_and_cash_equivalents / gross_interest_bearing_debt`
- `net_interest_bearing_debt = gross_interest_bearing_debt - cash_and_cash_equivalents`

Debt metrics bind every direct component explicitly. Total liabilities is not a
substitute for gross interest-bearing debt. No interest coverage, ROIC, score,
grade, or investment advice is produced.

The derived current-portion concept remains the gross-debt input, with lineage
to all three component Facts. Its latest values are 1,989,100; 6,982,400;
11,758,100; 10,147,900; and 3,913,300 万元 for FY2021—FY2025. The debt
exclusivity artifact records the aggregate, excluded long-term-payables row,
component pages, computed tie-out, and proof status; `mutually_exclusive` is
calculated from this evidence rather than asserted.

## Restatement and PIT behavior

The evidence review found four canonical changed direct facts: FY2022 total
liabilities; FY2023 total liabilities, full lease liabilities, and the current
lease component. The old FY2023 aggregate change is retained only as rejected
statement audit evidence. The runner emits the real FY2023 derived
current-portion v1→v2 chain; unchanged years receive no synthetic version.
FY2025 remains `not_yet_reviewable`.
