# PetroChina 2021—2025 Financial Safety Vertical Slice

Stage 2E-B records a transparent, descriptive financial-safety layer for
PetroChina (`601857.SH`). The evidence path is offline and dual-source: the
company annual report and the exchange-hosted annual report are checked against
the same direct CAS consolidated lines. Amounts are normalized from RMB million
to 万元 by ×100.

## Definitions

The additive Rule 006 concept set is:

- `total_liabilities`
- `short_term_borrowings`
- `current_portion_of_interest_bearing_non_current_liabilities`
- `long_term_borrowings`
- `bonds_payable`
- `lease_liabilities`
- `cash_and_cash_equivalents`

The four version-1, descriptive-only metrics are:

- `asset_liability_ratio = total_liabilities / total_assets`
- `gross_interest_bearing_debt = short_term_borrowings + current_portion + long_term_borrowings + bonds_payable + lease_liabilities`
- `cash_coverage_of_interest_bearing_debt = cash_and_cash_equivalents / gross_interest_bearing_debt`
- `net_interest_bearing_debt = gross_interest_bearing_debt - cash_and_cash_equivalents`

Debt metrics bind every direct component explicitly. Total liabilities is not a
substitute for gross interest-bearing debt. No interest coverage, ROIC, score,
grade, or investment advice is produced.

## Restatement and PIT behavior

The evidence review found four changed direct facts: FY2022 total liabilities;
FY2023 total liabilities, current portion, and lease liabilities. The runner
therefore emits FY2022 v1→v2 and FY2023 v1→v2 chains where the later filing
provides changed comparative evidence. Unchanged years do not receive a
synthetic version. FY2025 remains `not_yet_reviewable`.
